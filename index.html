Welcome home

Five lines, six generations,
one family.

The Hargrove Family Association has gathered the descendants of Eli and Mattie Hargrove every summer since 1978 — and kept everybody in touch the rest of the year. Whether this will be your thirtieth reunion or your first, you belong here.
