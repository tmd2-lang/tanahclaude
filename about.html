About the association

A family that decided to stay one.

In August 1978, after Eli Hargrove was laid to rest in Catonsville, eleven of his grandchildren sat in a church basement and admitted they had stopped being able to name each other’s children. Before they left that room they had a treasurer, a shoebox with $63 in it, and a promise to meet again the next July.

They have met every July since — forty‑eight summers, in eleven cities. The association now counts roughly 340 members across five lines and six generations, and the shoebox has become a scholarship fund.

Our purpose

To keep the descendants of Eli and Mattie Hargrove known to one another: to hold an annual reunion, to record and preserve the family’s history, to stand with one another in celebration and in grief, and to help the next generation get where it is going.

How we are organized

The five lines

Every member belongs to one of five lines, named for the five children of Eli and Mattie. Lines take turns hosting the reunion, and each line seats one delegate on the planning committee.
